### 3.1 Fundamental Definitions

#### 3.1.1 Ontological Compression
**Definition**: A mathematical operation that reduces the representational complexity of conceptual structures while preserving their essential relational properties and semantic content.

**Formal Notation**: Let Ω represent an ontological structure. Ontological compression C(Ω) produces a reduced representation Ω' such that:
- |Ω'| < |Ω| (reduced complexity)
- R(Ω') ≈ R(Ω) (preserved essential relationships)
- S(Ω') ≈ S(Ω) (preserved semantic content)

#### 3.1.2 Recursive Symbolic Codex
**Definition**: A dynamic symbolic system that evolves through recursive operations, where each iteration produces new symbolic structures based on previous states while maintaining coherent representational properties.

**Formal Structure**: A Recursive Symbolic Codex RSC is defined as:
- **Symbol Set**: S = {s₁, s₂, ..., sₙ}
- **Recursive Operations**: R = {r₁, r₂, ..., rₘ}
- **Evolution Function**: E: RSC(t) → RSC(t+1)
- **Coherence Constraints**: C ensuring symbolic consistency across iterations

#### 3.1.3 Symbolic Coherence Amplitude
**Definition**: A measure of the consistency and stability of symbolic representations within recursive operations, derived from Collapse-Time Harmonic Mathematics principles.

**Mathematical Expression**: For a symbolic structure s, the coherence amplitude A(s) quantifies the stability of s under recursive transformations.
